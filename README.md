## From operations to full-stack development

My career began in scaffolding and the skilled trades and grew into operations and sales leadership. Working close to the field, customers, and commercial decisions taught me to see how a problem in one part of a business affects the people and processes around it. That operating experience is the foundation of how I design systems.

As my work moved deeper into business systems, I began translating that knowledge into custom tools: making business rules explicit, organizing information, and turning data into something people could use to make decisions. Enterprise CRM architecture, sales intelligence, and custom analytics became ways to connect operational understanding with technical execution.

Building 50 applications has expanded that work across the full stack. I’ve developed my understanding of how interfaces, backend logic, data, integrations, and security fit together. My approach is hands-on and continuously evolving: learn the underlying concepts, build against real requirements, test the result, and improve the system as the business need becomes clearer.

## The expertise I bring
